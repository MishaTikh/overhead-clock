What the sign shows

Mode

Clock + planes shows the time and switches to the flight while a plane is audible. Planes only stays dark between planes. The middle button on the clock switches too.

City is where a landing plane came from, or where a departing plane is going. The left button on the clock cycles these.

Alerts

Clock screen extras

In clock mode, show these for a few seconds every so often. Leave them all off for just the time.

Colors

Brightness and text

The right button on the clock cycles brightness. Auto moves between the two levels below.

Quiet hours

No plane alerts from
to

During quiet hours the clock keeps showing the time but checks for no planes, which saves the free data allowance for daytime. Choose the same hour twice to turn quiet hours off.

Sensitivity

60 dBA is a plane you'd clearly notice over a quiet street (about 45 dBA). Raise it if the sign lights before you hear the plane; lower it if it misses planes you notice.

The free flight feed allows about 500 a day per home internet connection. Keep it lower if two clocks share one connection.
